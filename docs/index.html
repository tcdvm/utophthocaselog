Ophtho Case Log

A Chrome extension for veterinary students to log the ophthalmology cases they see on rotation, in a side panel beside ezyVet.

Open the panel next to a patient record and press Import / Add case. The patient's name and signalment are read from the record already on screen. A spider chart shows which of the seven areas of interest you have covered, so the gaps are visible while there is still time to fill them, and one button produces a PDF for your service chief.

The extension has no network access of any kind. Your case log is stored in your own browser on your own computer and is never transmitted anywhere.
