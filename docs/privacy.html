Privacy Policy

Ophtho Case Log · last updated 15 October 2026

Ophtho Case Log does not collect, transmit or store any data outside your own computer. There is no account, no server and no analytics.

What the extension reads

When you press Import / Add case while viewing a patient record in ezyVet, the extension reads five fields from the patient panel of that record:

  • patient name
  • species
  • breed
  • sex / neuter status
  • age

It reads nothing else. It does not read client or owner details, appointment schedules, medical record numbers, invoices or clinical notes.

It does not run on ezyVet pages at any other time. The extension declares no content script, so no code of ours is present on a page until you press the button; it runs once and is gone.

What the extension stores

Whatever you choose to save: the fields above, the date, the type of visit, your level of involvement in any procedure, the areas of interest you select and the case summary you write. It also stores your name, school and graduating term.

This is held in your browser's local storage, on your computer, in your Chrome profile. It is not synced to a Google account and it is not sent anywhere.

What the extension transmits

Nothing. The extension contains no networking code of any kind. It cannot send data to us or to anyone else, because it has no mechanism to do so.

This is enforced rather than asserted: an automated test (tests/privacy.test.mjs) fails the build if any connection API or web address appears anywhere in the source.

Where your data goes when you share it

The PDF report you generate is an ordinary file on your computer. Once you send it to someone, it is governed by whatever you and your institution agree — not by this policy. The report contains the patient names and signalment you logged, so treat it as you would any clinical record.

Deleting your data

Clear all in the panel permanently deletes the entire case log. Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes everything it stored.
